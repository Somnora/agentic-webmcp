# Ribband brand

## Name

Ribband is pronounced "RIB-band." The double `b` is intentional.

The name nods to the Blue Riband, the historic distinction associated with speed records on Atlantic passenger crossings. Ribband carries that idea into surfing the web: move quickly across authorized sources, preserve the evidence, and keep the final decision with the person.

Historical reference: [Speed and Luxury, Smithsonian National Museum of American History](https://americanhistory.si.edu/explore/exhibitions/on-the-water/online/ocean-crossings/comfort-courtesy-safety-speed/luxury)

## Product description

Ribband is an evidence-first decision workspace for people and web agents.

Use `Ribband` as the public product name. Use `Ribband WebMCP Challenge build` only when the hackathon context must be explicit. Do not expand Ribband into an acronym.

## Stable technical identifiers

The existing repository slug, Worker hostnames, package name, environment variables, and `X-Agentic-*` compatibility headers remain unchanged for release stability. They are infrastructure identifiers, not the public product name.

## Visual mark

The primary lockup pairs two forward-flowing black ribbons with a lowercase `ribband` wordmark. The standalone mark is a ribbon-drawn `R` with an open, accelerating terminal. Use the full lockup for identity-bearing surfaces such as the application header and the standalone mark for compact surfaces such as the favicon or app icon.

The approved artwork is monochrome black. Forest, ivory, and bronze remain the supporting application palette, but do not recolor, redraw, stretch, shadow, or place effects behind the logo. The application-ready transparent exports are `public/ribband-lockup.png` and `public/ribband-symbol.png`.

## Voice

Ribband should sound precise, calm, and useful. Lead with the result. Show sources and uncertainty. State where automation stops. Avoid speed claims that are not measured and avoid language that suggests the agent can approve, book, order, or pay.
